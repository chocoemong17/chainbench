This log connects reported needs to observable changes. A shipped improvement
does not by itself establish better learning outcomes. The [classroom record](CLASSROOM_PILOT.md)
retains all survey distributions and collection limits.

## Reporting a new problem

Use the [lesson feedback form](https://github.com/chocoemong17/chainbench/issues/new?template=lesson_feedback.yml)
for a specific confusing step, or the bug form for a reproducible malfunction.
Include the topic, language and relevant timestamp or control. Share aggregate
classroom observations if useful; do not upload student names or individual records.

New topics begin with a rough concept and difficulty review. Polishing starts only
after that review; four scenes are not a required format. See [Contributing](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
