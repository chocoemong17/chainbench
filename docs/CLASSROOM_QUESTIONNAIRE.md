# Classroom questionnaire: English reading translation

[Classroom context and limitations](CLASSROOM_PILOT.md) · [Original Korean wording and aggregate JSON](data/classroom-survey-150.json)

The survey was supplied in Korean. The English wording below is a reading
translation, not a separately administered or validated English questionnaire.
Every original choice is retained in its original order, including zero counts.
Each question has 150 responses and one choice per response. The counts are copied
from the public aggregate; no student-level records are included.

These choices describe perceived understanding, interest and preferences after
use of earlier material. They are not test scores or evidence of learning gains,
and they do not evaluate the subsequently added Backpropagation, CNN or Dropout.
