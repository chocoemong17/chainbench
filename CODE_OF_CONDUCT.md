# Code of Conduct

ChainBench is a technical open-source project. Contributors are expected to keep
discussion focused on the work and to treat other participants with respect.

## Expected behavior

- Give technical feedback about ideas and code rather than about people.
- Be precise about evidence, especially when discussing published results.
- Welcome corrections and requests for reproducibility details.
- Respect privacy: do not post another person's private information or unpublished work.
- Avoid harassment, insults, threats, discriminatory remarks, and persistent disruption.

## Maintainer response

The maintainer may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate these
expectations, and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the project
safe and productive.

For sensitive reports that should not be public, do not include private data in
an issue. Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting if it is enabled; otherwise
contact the maintainer through an appropriate private channel.
